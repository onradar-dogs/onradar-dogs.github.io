Step-by-Step: How to Groom a Dachshund (Community Way)

We’ve synthesised knowledge from hundreds of Dachshund owners. Follow these stages, and adapt to your dog’s personality.

1. Ear Care — The Floppy Ear Check

Dachshund ears are warm, dark, and prone to infections. Clean gently with a vet-approved solution. “Our tribe says: if it smells like corn chips, it’s time to clean.”

2. Brushing — More Than Just Hair

Smooth: rubber mitt weekly. Longhair: daily with a pin brush. Wirehair: stripping every few months. Brushing is communication — it’s when your Dachshund feels safest.

3. Nail Trimming — The Non-Negotiable

Use guillotine clippers. Forget the 'quick' stress — go slowly, reward, and if unsure, watch a tutorial from a fellow owner. Some of the best how to groom a dachshund ideas come from the community: use a scratch board to file naturally.

4. Bathing — The Great Sausage Wash

Only every 6–8 weeks. Use oatmeal shampoo. Dry thoroughly — especially under the neck and between paw pads. How to groom a dachshund activities include post-bath zoomie watching.

🐾 Insider fact: Many owners use a silicone brush in the bath — it feels like a massage and reduces shedding. One of the timeless how to groom a dachshund facts: they hate water but love the warmth.
