    What is IVDD and why are Dachshunds at higher risk?

    IVDD (Intervertebral Disc Disease) is a spinal condition where disc cushions slip or burst. Dachshunds are genetically prone due to their long backs and short legs. The community often calls it the “wiener dog back problem.” The IVDD in Dachshunds: Complete Guide explains that their chondrodystrophic (short-legged) genes cause early disc degeneration.
