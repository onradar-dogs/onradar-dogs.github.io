Dachshund Health Overview Facts – What Every Owner Should Know

  • IVDD is the #1 concern: Over 25% of dachshunds will suffer intervertebral disc disease. Keep them lean and use ramps, not stairs.
  • Dental disease affects 80% by age 3: Daily brushing can prevent gum infections that harm hearts.
  • Weight is a community issue: A healthy doxie should show a slight waistline. Overweight dogs have 4x more back problems.
  • Swimming is a tribe favorite: Hydrotherapy strengthens muscles without pounding the spine.
