Dachshund Breed Guides

Temperament, coat, health and breeding: the Dachshund reference pages on this site.

Back to latest

Start here if you are deciding whether a Dachshund suits your home, or want to understand the dog you already have. The overview and temperament guides explain the breed's hound instincts and stubborn streak; the colour and shedding pages cover coat types and what grooming each needs.

The health pages go deeper: the long back makes IVDD the breed's best-known risk, and the screening guide lists the DNA and orthopaedic tests worth asking a breeder about. If you are thinking about a litter, read the two breeding guides first.
